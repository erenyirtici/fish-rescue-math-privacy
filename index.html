Privacy Policy for Fish Rescue Math

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Fish Rescue Math is developed and published by Preda Interactive. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Fish Rescue Math does not directly ask users to provide personal information such as their name, address, phone number, or email address.

The game may use third-party services provided by Google. These services may process certain information automatically as necessary to provide their features, subject to their own privacy policies and settings.

2. Google Play Games Services

Fish Rescue Math may use Google Play Games Services for gameplay-related features such as sign-in, leaderboards, achievements, and player progress.

Information associated with these features is processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies and the user's Google account settings.

3. Analytics, Advertising, and In-App Purchases

Future versions of Fish Rescue Math may include services such as Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing.

If these services are enabled in a released version, this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play data disclosures will be updated as necessary to reflect the data practices of the current version of the game.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed through enabled third-party services may be used to provide game functionality, maintain leaderboards and achievements, understand game performance and usage, improve the game, prevent abuse, display advertising where applicable, and process in-app purchases where applicable.

5. Data Sharing

Preda Interactive does not sell users' personal information.

Data may be processed by third-party service providers only when their services are integrated into the game and are necessary to provide the related functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Fish Rescue Math does not currently maintain a separate user account database. Information handled by Google services is retained and managed according to Google's policies and the user's Google account settings.

For questions or requests concerning information directly controlled by Preda Interactive, users may contact us using the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

Fish Rescue Math is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. Preda Interactive does not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the game.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the game. Third-party services integrated into the game are responsible for protecting information they process according to their own security and privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when game features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact
